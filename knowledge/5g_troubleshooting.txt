5G TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

5G network performance depends on signal strength, network congestion, device compatibility, spectrum availability and backhaul capacity.

If 5G internet is slow, first check whether the device is connected to 5G. Check signal strength and perform a speed test.

High latency can make browsing, gaming and video calls feel slow even when download speed appears acceptable.

Packet loss can cause buffering, dropped calls, unstable connections and poor application performance.

If download speed is low but signal is strong, network congestion or backhaul limitations may be responsible.

If signal strength is weak, move to an area with better coverage or move closer to a window or outdoor location.

Restarting the device can refresh the network registration.

Users should verify that their device and SIM support the required 5G bands.

If a local tower has an active outage, customer-side troubleshooting may not resolve the issue until the network problem is repaired.

For persistent network problems, collect speed, latency, packet-loss and signal information and create a support ticket.
