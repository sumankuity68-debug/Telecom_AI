BILLING POLICY

A telecom bill can contain the base plan charge, add-on charges, roaming charges and applicable taxes.

The total bill amount is the sum of these components.

A pending bill means payment has not yet been recorded as completed.

An overdue bill requires payment according to the applicable account policy.

Customers can request an explanation of individual bill components.

A support agent should not claim that a charge is incorrect without checking the billing record.

For disputed charges, create a billing support ticket when investigation is required.

Financial actions such as payment, recharge or refunds require explicit customer confirmation and appropriate authorization.
