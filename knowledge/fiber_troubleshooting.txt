FIBER TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

For fiber broadband problems, first check whether the modem or optical network terminal has power.

Check whether the optical or PON indicator is normal.

A red LOS indicator can indicate loss of optical signal.

Restart the router and optical network equipment.

Check Ethernet or Wi-Fi connectivity between the customer device and router.

If the router is connected but internet access is unavailable, check whether there is an active service outage.

Poor Wi-Fi performance can occur even when the fiber connection itself is healthy.

A wired speed test can help separate Wi-Fi problems from broadband connection problems.

Persistent optical signal problems should be escalated to technical support.
