SIM TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

If a SIM has no network service, first restart the mobile device.

Check whether airplane mode is enabled.

Remove and reinsert the SIM if the device supports a removable SIM.

Check whether the SIM is correctly detected by the device.

Verify that the account is active and not suspended.

If mobile data is not working while calls work, check the mobile data setting and APN configuration.

If the SIM works in another compatible device, the original device may require troubleshooting.

If the SIM does not work in multiple compatible devices, the SIM or account may require further support.

For repeated SIM registration problems, create a support ticket and provide the customer's account information.
